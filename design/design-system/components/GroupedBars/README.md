# Grouped bars

Use grouped bars to compare two or three measures across partners or sides, like revenue, expenses and net contribution, on one shared axis.

- Bars are 16 px, 4 px apart inside a group; series colours follow `chart-1`, `chart-2`, `chart-3` in that order; a value below zero hangs under the axis in `chart-negative` and is written under the group name.
- Always show a legend above the plot and give every bar an `aria-label`; the detailed table below the chart carries the exact figures.
