How partner loans work

A loan is money a partner lends the company. It sits under liabilities on the balance sheet, not in the partner’s capital account, so it never changes equity or profit share.

Repayments go back to the partner who lent. When the outstanding amount reaches ₹0 the loan closes on its own.
