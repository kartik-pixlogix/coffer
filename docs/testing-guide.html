1Before you start

  1. Reset the demo data: php artisan migrate:fresh --seed. This puts every figure in this guide back to its starting value. Run it again whenever a test changes the data and you want to start over.
  2. Start the app with composer run dev (web server, queue worker and Vite). Stop and restart it if it was already running, because the queue worker has to listen on the new queues (exports, imports, mail, vault).
  3. Open http://localhost:8000. Use a normal or incognito Chrome window.
  4. Keep a terminal open for php artisan coffer:demo-2fa, which prints the current 2FA code for the demo accounts. Codes change every 30 seconds.
  5. Check the books balance at any point: php artisan coffer:ledger-integrity should print “All 4 companies balance”.

2Sign-in, 2FA and company switching

Pages: /login, /register, /forgot-password, company chip in the top bar
  1. Sign in as Aarav. Enter the code from coffer:demo-2fa when asked.
  2. Use the company chip in the top bar to switch between the four companies.
  3. Sign out, then try a wrong password five times in a row.
  4. Register a brand-new account from /register?plan=starter (use any email). Walk through the company wizard (single company, then try a JV).
  5. Leave a tab idle long enough for the session to expire (or clear cookies), then click something.
ExpectAarav lands on the Dashboard. Each company switch reloads the page with that company's data only. Repeated wrong passwords are throttled. A new account gets a 14-day trial on the chosen plan and its own empty workspace. An expired session shows the “session expired” dialog instead of a raw error.

6Team and salaries

Pages: /team, /team/{member}, “Run salaries” button
  1. Switch between the partner tabs and check member cards (rate or monthly cost, billable hours, earnings).
  2. Add a member: try both Hourly and Fixed and link a user.
  3. Edit a member's rate. An “Effective from” date appears; pick 1 Nov 2026.
  4. Open a member's profile: rate history, monthly hours, salary history and vault access.
  5. Click Run salaries, pick October 2026 and look at the preview, then run it.
  6. Deactivate a member, then reactivate. Try deleting a member who has hours logged.
ExpectRate changes only affect hours from the effective date. Running salaries for an unchanged month does nothing (“unchanged”). If hours changed, the old salary entry is reversed and a new one posted. Each run also shows as a read-only Auto row in Expenses. Members with history can't be deleted, only deactivated. Rohan (Member) only sees his own profile.

7Timesheets and import

Pages: /timesheets, /timesheets/import

Logging hours

  1. Click Log hours → Day mode: pick a member, project and date, enter 2:30, add a note, save.
  2. Log hours again in Month mode for November with 160 h, with and without “Include Saturdays”.
  3. Log 13 h for one person on one day.
  4. Filter the entries by member, partner, project, billable and search. Page through (25 per page).
  5. Delete one entry from a month batch. The confirm offers “Delete all N entries in this batch”.
Expect2:30 saves as 2.5 h. Month mode shows “N working days → x.xx hrs/day” and the split adds up exactly to the total. 13 h gives a warning, not an error. Toast: “2.5 h logged for {Name}”.

Bulk import

  1. On /timesheets/import, download the template, fill a few rows (mix 2026-11-03, 03/11/2026, 3-Nov-26, a monthly row 2026-10, a misspelled member name, a 30 h row), upload it.
  2. Map the columns, preview, use the All / OK / Errors chips, download the error CSV, then import.
  3. Try the Monthly tab: add rows per member with a monthly total, save.
ExpectEvery date format is understood. The misspelled name says “Did you mean …?”. The 30 h row is rejected. The import runs in the background and finishes with a toast like “N entries imported · x h added”. Unknown projects are created.

9Expenses and approvals

Page: /expenses (try /expenses?month=2026-10 for salary Auto rows)
  1. Check the status chips: there are 3 pending and 1 rejected expense in the demo data.
  2. Add an expense with Custom split between two partners; type amounts that don't add up, then fix them. Attach a receipt image. Use “+ Add category” inline.
  3. Add one paid by Rohan's personal card, one with Half, one paid by the company directly.
  4. As Aarav, open a pending expense submitted by Ishita and approve it. Reject another with a reason.
  5. As Ishita, try to approve an expense you submitted yourself.
  6. Select several pending rows and use bulk approve / reject. Export the filtered list to CSV.
  7. Open the receipt drawer: details, payer lines, receipt preview, approval history.
  8. As an Admin, edit an approved expense.
ExpectSave is blocked until a custom split adds up exactly. Nothing hits the books until approval; rejecting needs a reason and posts nothing. Partners can't approve their own expenses (Admins can). Bulk actions skip rows you can't decide and say how many. Editing an approved expense reverses it and sends it back to Pending. Auto salary rows can't be edited.
Decision D47 [VERIFY]: every expense needs approval (the phone design hinted that expenses under ₹10,000 count straight away).

12Partner loans

  2. Record a new loan (new money). Record a repayment; try one larger than what's outstanding; use “Pay in full”.

17Credentials Vault

Pages: /vault, /vault/items/create, /vault/requests, /vault/health, /vault/audit, /vault/policies, /vault/import, /vault/export

Lock, reveal and copy

  1. As Aarav (Saarthi Portal JV) open /vault. It starts locked: unlock with a 2FA code.
  2. Open the GitHub item. Click Reveal and Copy on the password; click Show code for the one-time code.
  3. Wait 30 seconds after revealing. Leave the vault idle for 15 minutes.
  4. Sign in as Meera (no 2FA) and open the vault.
Expect4 vaults and 13 demo items. Revealing asks for a fresh code if the last one is older than 15 minutes. The value hides itself after 30 s and the clipboard is cleared. After 15 idle minutes the vault locks again. Meera is asked to turn on 2FA first. Every reveal, copy and code use appears in the audit log.

Add, edit, history

  1. Add item → pick a provider tile (e.g. GitLab) → fill the form; use the password generator and watch the strength meter. Also try /vault/items/create?type=server.
  2. Edit the item, change the password, open History and restore an older version.
  3. Open the AWS item marked “Admins only” as Ishita.

Sharing, requests, health, audit, policies

  1. Share an item with Ishita at Use for 7 days; change it to View; remove it.
  2. As Kabir, “Ask for access” on an item; as Aarav, approve it on /vault/requests (2 pending and 1 denied are seeded).
  3. /vault/health: check the score and the weak / reused / old / expiring / breached lists; click Rotate on one.
  4. /vault/audit: filter by person and action; export CSV.
  5. /vault/policies: change idle lock to 5 minutes, save, and see it take effect.

Import and export

  1. /vault/import: upload tests/Fixtures/vault-import/bitwarden.json or one of the CSVs there; preview; import.
  2. /vault/export: enter a passphrase and a fresh code; download the file; import it back.
ExpectDuplicates are skipped and reported. The export file is encrypted and imports back intact (attachments are not included). No page ever shows a secret until you reveal it.
Decisions to confirm: D68 (Admins can't open personal vaults), D70 (health score weights), D72 (“Use” can reveal), D76 (default lock settings), D77 (exports exclude attachments).

24Sign-off checklist

  • Demo figures match: income ₹62,10,000 · expenses ₹43,67,650 · Vertex Labs owes Bluefin ₹3,24,600
  • php artisan coffer:ledger-integrity prints “All 4 companies balance” after your tests

  • Reports, Dashboard, Trial balance, Balance sheet and Capital agree for the same period
  • PDF and Excel exports open correctly
  • Plan limit opens the Upgrade window; Stripe test-mode trial converts to paid (docs/BILLING_TESTING.md)
  • Marketing pages render with styling, light and dark, desktop and phone
  • /admin rejects customer accounts; impersonation shows the banner and blocks the vault
  • Phone layout at 390 px on the main pages; no sideways scrolling
  • Decisions marked [VERIFY] in docs/DECISIONS.md reviewed and answered
